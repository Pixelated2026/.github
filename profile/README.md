## `01 / ABOUT_US.TXT`

We're **Pixelated** — a small crew of designers, developers, and habitual button-pushers.

We build games, software, digital worlds, and things you didn't know you needed to click.

We like projects that spark curiosity, reward exploration, and make the digital world a little less ordinary.

No corporate operating system detected. Just a few curious humans making things worth playing with.

> **Serious about play. Not so serious about ourselves.**

## `05 / OUR_PHILOSOPHY`

We don't think everything needs to be serious to be meaningful.

We believe in:

- **Curiosity** — explore ideas just because they're interesting.
- **Creativity** — make things that have their own personality.
- **Experimentation** — try it, break it, try again.
- **Play** — technology should be fun, too.
- **Originality** — don't just make another version of the same thing.
- **Community** — great things are better when shared.

```text
> think differently
> build something strange
> make it playable
> repeat
```
